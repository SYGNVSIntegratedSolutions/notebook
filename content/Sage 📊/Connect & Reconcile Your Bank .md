### ➡️ **Why Connect & Reconcile Your Bank?**

Linking your bank lets Sage automatically import transactions, so you can:

- ✅ Keep your books accurate.
    
- 🔍 Easily spot missing payments or errors.
    
- 🕒 Save time — no manual entry needed!
    

---

### 🔌 **A) Connect Your Bank Account**

1 Log in to **Sage Accounting**.  
2 Go to the **Banking** or **Bank Accounts** section.  
3 Click **Connect Bank Account** (or **Add Bank Account**).  
4 Choose your bank from the list.  
5 Log in securely with your online banking credentials.  
6 Grant Sage permission to import transactions.  
✅ Sage will start syncing transactions daily.

---

### 📥 **B) Import Bank Transactions**

- Transactions will appear automatically under your bank account in Sage.
    
- They usually show up within 1-2 business days.
    

---

### 🔍 **C) Reconcile Transactions**

1 Go to **Banking → Reconcile** or **Transactions**.  
2 You’ll see a list of imported bank transactions.  
3 Match each transaction to an existing invoice, bill, or expense:

- ✅ If Sage finds a match, approve it.
    
- ❓ If no match, create a new entry or investigate.  
    4 If there are fees or interest, enter them manually as needed.  
    5 When all transactions are matched, your bank account is reconciled.
    

---

### 🧾 **D) Fix Discrepancies**

- If your bank balance doesn’t match Sage’s, check for:
    
    - Missed transactions
        
    - Duplicates
        
    - Wrong amounts
        
- Adjust or add missing entries until balances match.
    

---

### 💡 **Tips for Smooth Reconciliation**

✅ Reconcile your bank at least weekly — catch issues early.  
🔍 Use filters to find unmatched transactions fast.  
📁 Keep receipts handy for any unclear charges.  
🔒 Ensure your bank login info stays secure.
