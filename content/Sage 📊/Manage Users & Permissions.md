
### ➡️ **Why Manage Users?**

Giving the right access keeps your data safe and lets your team work efficiently without mistakes or accidental changes.

---

### 🏁 **A) Open User Management**

1 Log in to **Sage Accounting** as an admin.  
2 Go to **Settings** (⚙️ icon).  
3 Click **User Management** or **Manage Users**.

---

### ➕ **B) Add a New User**

1 Click **Add User** or **Invite User**.  
2 Enter their email address.  
3 Choose their role/permission level:

- 🛠️ **Admin** — full access
    
- 📊 **Accountant** — can manage finances
    
- 🧾 **Sales** — invoicing only
    
- 👀 **Read-only** — view reports but no changes  
    4 Click **Send Invite** — user will get an email to set up their login.
    

---

### 🔄 **C) Edit or Remove Users**

1 In **User Management**, find the user you want to update.  
2 Click **Edit** to change their role or details.  
3 Click **Remove** or **Deactivate** if they should no longer access Sage.

---

### 🔒 **D) Best Practices for Permissions**

- ✅ Only give **Admin** rights to trusted people.
    
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Assign roles based on job functions — no one needs full access if they don’t handle everything.
    
- 🔄 Review user access regularly to keep it up to date.
    

---

### 💡 **Tips**

- Use **two-factor authentication (2FA)** if available for extra security.
    
- Train your team on how to use Sage responsibly.
    
- Keep contact info updated for quick support.
