
### ➡️ **Why Run Reports?**

Reports show how your business is doing — profits, expenses, cash flow — helping you make smart decisions. Sage makes it easy to get clear, accurate reports anytime! 📈💼

---

### 🏁 **A) Open the Reports Section**

1️⃣ Log in to **Sage Accounting**.  
2️⃣ Click **Reports** from the main menu.

---

### 🔍 **B) Choose Your Report**

Common reports:

- 📈 **Profit & Loss Statement** (Income vs Expenses)
    
- 📉 **Balance Sheet** (Assets, Liabilities, Equity)
    
- 🕒 **Aged Receivables** (Who owes you money & how long)
    
- 🕒 **Aged Payables** (What you owe vendors & due dates)
    
- 💵 **Cash Flow Statement**
    

---

### 🗓️ **C) Set Date Range**

1️⃣ Select the period you want to review:

- This month
    
- Last quarter
    
- Custom dates (e.g., Jan 1 to Jun 30)  
    2️⃣ Click **Apply** or **Run Report**.
    

---

### 📄 **D) View and Analyze**

- The report will display on screen with charts or tables.
    
- Look for key numbers: total income, expenses, net profit.
    
- Use filters to drill down (e.g., by department or project if available).
    

---

### 💾 **E) Export or Print**

1️⃣ To share or save the report:

- Click **Export** → choose **PDF** or **Excel**.
    
- Or click **Print** to get a paper copy.  
    2️⃣ Save reports regularly for your records or accountant.
    

---

### 💡 **Tips for Using Reports**

✅ Run reports monthly to track progress.  
📊 Compare periods to spot trends (growth or issues).  
🧾 Use reports to prepare tax returns or financial statements.  
🔍 Ask your accountant which reports matter most for your business.
