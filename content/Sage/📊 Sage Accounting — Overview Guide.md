### ➡️ **What is Sage?**

**Sage** is accounting software that helps you manage:

- 💰 **Money In** (sales & invoices)
    
- 💸 **Money Out** (bills & payments)
    
- 🧾 **Taxes** (VAT/GST returns)
    
- 📊 **Reports** (profit & loss, balance sheet)
    
- 👥 **Customers & Vendors**
    
- 🏦 **Bank Reconciliation**
    

Perfect for small to medium businesses that need organized, compliant books!

---

### 📝 **1️⃣ Add Customers & Vendors**

1️⃣ Go to **Contacts** → choose **Customers** or **Suppliers**.  
2️⃣ Click **Add New**.  
3️⃣ Fill in: name, address, email, payment terms, etc.  
4️⃣ 💾 Click **Save** — now you can bill or pay them easily.

---

### 💼 **2️⃣ Create & Send Invoices**

1️⃣ Go to **Sales** → **Invoices** → **Create Invoice**.  
2️⃣ Pick a customer.  
3️⃣ Add:

- 📦 Products/services
    
- 🔢 Quantities & prices
    
- 📅 Due date  
    4️⃣ Click **Save & Send** — email it straight to your customer!
    

---

### 💸 **3️⃣ Record Bills & Expenses**

1️⃣ Go to **Purchases** → **Bills** → **New Bill**.  
2️⃣ Pick a supplier.  
3️⃣ Add:

- 🧾 Items or services purchased
    
- 💰 Amounts
    
- 📅 Due date  
    4️⃣ Click **Save** — Sage tracks what you owe.
    

---

### 🏦 **4️⃣ Connect & Reconcile Your Bank**

1️⃣ Go to **Banking** → **Connect Bank Account**.  
2️⃣ Pick your bank & sign in securely.  
3️⃣ Sage imports transactions daily.  
4️⃣ 🔍 Go to **Reconcile**, match transactions to invoices/bills.  
5️⃣ ✅ Confirm matches — your books stay accurate!

---

### 📊 **5️⃣ Run Financial Reports**

1️⃣ Go to **Reports**.  
2️⃣ Common reports:

- 📈 Profit & Loss (income & expenses)
    
- 📉 Balance Sheet (assets & liabilities)
    
- 📑 Aged Receivables/Payables  
    3️⃣ Choose dates → click **Run Report** → export as PDF/Excel.
    

---

### 🧾 **6️⃣ Handle Tax Returns**

1️⃣ Go to **Taxes/VAT** → check your sales & purchase tax.  
2️⃣ Sage calculates how much to pay or reclaim.  
3️⃣ 📤 Submit directly to your tax authority (if integrated).  
4️⃣ 📁 Save returns for your records.

---

### 🔑 **7️⃣ Manage Users & Permissions**

1️⃣ Go to **Settings** → **User Management**.  
2️⃣ Add users → choose roles:

- Admin: full access
    
- Accountant: mostly financial tasks
    
- Sales: invoices only  
    3️⃣ ✅ Save — everyone gets the right level of access!
    

---

### 💡 **Pro Tips for Sage**

✅ Keep data tidy — reconcile banks weekly.  
🧾 Attach receipts to expenses for easy audits.  
📨 Send invoices fast — get paid faster!  
🔒 Back up regularly (if using desktop versions).
