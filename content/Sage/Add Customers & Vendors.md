
### ➡️ **Why Add Contacts?**

Adding **Customers** (who pay you) and **Vendors/Suppliers** (you pay them) helps you:

- 💰 Invoice and get paid quickly.
    
- 💸 Record bills and expenses accurately.
    
- 🔍 Track who owes what — no more sticky notes!
    

---

### 🏷️ **A) Find the Contacts Section**

1️⃣ Log in to **Sage Accounting**.  
2️⃣ Look at the top or side menu → Click **Contacts**.  
3️⃣ Choose:

- 👥 **Customers** (people/businesses you sell to)
    
- 🏢 **Suppliers/Vendors** (people/businesses you buy from)
    

---

### ➕ **B) Add a New Customer**

1️⃣ Click **Add New Customer** (sometimes called **New Contact**).  
2️⃣ Fill in:

- 📛 **Name** — business or person
    
- 📬 **Address** — billing address
    
- 📧 **Email** — for sending invoices
    
- 📞 **Phone** — for contact
    
- 🧾 **Payment Terms** — e.g., Net 30 (due in 30 days)
    

---

### 🏷️ **C) Add a New Vendor/Supplier**

1️⃣ Click **Suppliers** (or Vendors).  
2️⃣ Click **Add New Supplier**.  
3️⃣ Fill in:

- 📛 Name (business/person)
    
- 📬 Address (where payments go)
    
- 📧 Email (send purchase orders or queries)
    
- 🧾 Default payment terms — e.g., Pay within 14 days
    

---

### 🗂️ **D) Add Extra Details (Optional)**

📌 For both customers & vendors, you can add:

- 💳 Bank details (for direct payments)
    
- 💼 Tax ID or VAT Number
    
- 📝 Notes — special instructions or account manager
    

---

### 💾 **E) Save & Done!**

1️⃣ Double-check the info.  
2️⃣ Click **Save** (or **Save & Close**).  
3️⃣ ✅ Your contact is now ready — you can invoice them or record bills anytime.

---

### 🔍 **F) Edit or Find Later**

1️⃣ Go back to **Contacts**.  
2️⃣ Use the search bar to find a name fast.  
3️⃣ Click a contact to **Edit**, **Archive**, or **View Transaction History**.

---

### 💡 **Tips for Good Contacts**

✔️ Keep names clear — use full company names.  
✔️ Use email fields — so Sage can send invoices/bills automatically.  
✔️ Add payment terms now — saves time later!  
✔️ Archive old contacts instead of deleting — keeps your records tidy.
