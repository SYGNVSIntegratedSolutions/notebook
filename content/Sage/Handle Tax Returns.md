
### ➡️ **Why Handle Taxes in Sage?**

Sage helps you calculate and file your VAT/GST or sales taxes so you stay compliant and avoid penalties. It keeps track of taxes on sales and purchases automatically! ✅💼

---

### 🏁 **A) Go to the Taxes Section**

1️⃣ Log in to **Sage Accounting**.  
2️⃣ Click **Taxes** or **VAT** from the main menu.

---

### 📊 **B) Review Your Tax Summary**

- Sage shows a summary of:
    
    - Sales tax collected on invoices
        
    - Purchase tax paid on bills
        
    - Tax due or reclaimable
        
- Check the date range matches your tax reporting period.
    

---

### 🧾 **C) Prepare Your Tax Return**

1️⃣ Click **Prepare Return** or **Create Tax Return**.  
2️⃣ Sage fills in most fields automatically based on your recorded transactions.  
3️⃣ Review carefully — make sure amounts look right.

---

### 📤 **D) Submit Your Tax Return**

- If your country supports it, you can **submit directly** from Sage:  
    1️⃣ Click **Submit Return**.  
    2️⃣ Follow prompts to connect to your tax authority’s system.
    
- If not, **export a PDF** or CSV to upload manually on the tax agency’s website.
    

---

### 📁 **E) Save & Archive**

- Save a copy of the return for your records.
    
- Attach any supporting documents (invoices, bills).
    
- Set reminders for the next tax deadline.
    

---

### 💡 **Tips for Smooth Tax Filing**

✅ Enter all invoices and bills before filing — ensures accurate tax amounts.  
📅 File on time to avoid fines!  
📞 Contact your accountant if unsure about any details.  
🔄 Keep Sage updated with latest tax rates if they change.
