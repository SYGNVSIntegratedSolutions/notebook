### **Top 10 Cyber Threat Incident Response Recommendations**

_(From Your Trusted IT Partner)_

1. **Create a Written Incident Response Plan (IRP)**
    

- Document who does what when a cyber incident happens — include clear steps, escalation paths, and contact lists.
    

2. **Identify an Incident Response Team**
    

- Assign roles: internal IT, executive leadership, legal, PR, and third-party experts like your managed IT firm.
    

3. **Establish Clear Communication Channels**
    

- Set up secure, out-of-band ways to communicate during an attack (e.g., secure messaging app or alternative phones).
    

4. **Detect and Contain Quickly**
    

- Use up-to-date antivirus, EDR (Endpoint Detection & Response), and 24/7 monitoring to detect threats early and isolate affected systems fast.
    

5. **Back Up Critical Data — And Test Restores**
    

- Ensure backups are recent, offline, and tested regularly so you can recover quickly if data is encrypted or destroyed.
    

6. **Document Everything During an Incident**
    

- Record timelines, what was affected, actions taken, and evidence. This is critical for recovery, insurance, and legal reporting.
    

7. **Notify Relevant Stakeholders Promptly**
    

- Inform leadership, your IT partner, cyber insurance, and legal counsel as soon as possible. Know any breach notification laws that may apply.
    

8. **Work with Trusted Experts**
    

- Engage your IT firm or a specialized incident response provider for containment, forensics, and safe recovery.
    

9. **Update Credentials After an Incident**
    

- Require password resets and enable MFA to prevent repeat compromise.
    

10. **Review and Learn Afterward**
    

- Perform a post-incident review. Identify root causes, fix gaps, and update your IRP so the same threat can’t succeed twice.
    

---

### **Bonus Pro Tips**

**Have a Cyber Insurance Policy**

- Ensure it covers response costs, legal fees, and business interruption. Know how to make a claim.
    

**Run Tabletop Exercises Annually**

- Practice “what if” scenarios with your team and your IT firm to keep everyone ready.
    

**Keep Contact Info Handy**

- Store your IT partner’s emergency number, key vendors, and legal counsel contacts where they’re easy to find (offline too!).
