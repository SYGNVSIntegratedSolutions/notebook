### **Top 10 Acceptable Use Recommendations for Company Phones & Laptops** 📱

1. **Use Strong, Unique Passwords or Passcodes**
    
    - It’s recommended to secure devices with strong, unique passwords or PINs, and to change them regularly.
        
2. **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**
    
    - Whenever possible, employees are encouraged to turn on MFA for company accounts accessed through phones or laptops.
        
3. **Keep Devices Updated**
    
    - Regularly install operating system updates, security patches, and approved software updates to protect against vulnerabilities.
        
4. **Use Company-Approved Apps and Software Only**
    
    - It’s best to install and use only trusted, authorized applications to avoid unintentional malware.
        
5. **Connect Only to Trusted Networks**
    
    - Employees are encouraged to avoid using public Wi-Fi without a secure VPN when accessing company data.
        
6. **Store Sensitive Data Securely**
    
    - Save work files to approved cloud storage or secure network drives rather than on local devices whenever practical.
        
7. **Be Alert for Phishing and Malicious Links**
    
    - Employees should be cautious with emails, texts, and messages — verify suspicious requests and avoid clicking unknown links.
        
8. **Report Lost or Stolen Devices Immediately**
    
    - If a device is lost or stolen, notify IT support promptly so they can help protect company data.
        
9. **Limit Personal Use**
    
    - Personal activities should be kept to a reasonable minimum to reduce security risks and protect company resources.
        
10. **Lock Devices When Not in Use**
    

- It’s good practice to lock screens when stepping away, even briefly, to prevent unauthorized access.
