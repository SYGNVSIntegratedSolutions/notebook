### **Top 10 Steps for Business Continuity During Natural Disasters** 🌩️

1. **Develop a Written Business Continuity Plan (BCP)**
    

- Document step-by-step actions for hurricanes, floods, and tornadoes. Include key contacts and recovery priorities.
    

2. **Back Up Data Off-Site and in the Cloud**
    

- Keep critical files and systems backed up to secure off-site/cloud storage that’s accessible if local servers are damaged.
    

3. **Establish a Remote Work Plan**
    

- Ensure employees have laptops, VPN access, and procedures for working remotely if your office is unsafe or inaccessible.
    

4. **Maintain an Updated Emergency Contact List**
    

- Include employees, vendors, clients, and local emergency services. Store digitally and on paper.
    

5. **Identify Alternate Work Locations**
    

- Secure agreements for temporary office space or co-working hubs outside high-risk flood or hurricane zones.
    

6. **Protect Physical Assets**
    

- Elevate equipment where possible, secure important documents in waterproof containers, and board up windows before storms.
    

7. **Test Your Backup Power Systems**
    

- Have generators ready and test them regularly to keep essential systems running during outages.
    

8. **Train Employees on Emergency Procedures**
    

- Run annual drills covering evacuation, shelter-in-place, and remote work scenarios.
    

9. **Review Insurance Coverage**
    

- Make sure your policies cover hurricanes, flood damage, and business interruption — gaps can be costly.
    

10. **Coordinate with Local Authorities**
    

- Stay connected with local emergency management offices for real-time updates and evacuation guidance.
    

---

### 🌧️ **Bonus Local Tips** 🌧️

**Hurricane Season Preparedness**

- Pre-stage supplies (fuel, batteries, tarps, food, water) well before June — the Gulf hurricane season can ramp up fast.
    

**Flood Mitigation**

- If you’re in a flood-prone area, consider installing flood barriers or sandbags in advance of heavy rains.
    

**Communication Continuity**

- Use mass notification systems (text, email, phone trees) to keep staff and clients informed during severe weather.
