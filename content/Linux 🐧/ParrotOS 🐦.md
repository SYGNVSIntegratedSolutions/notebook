# **Why Consider Switching from Windows to Parrot OS?**

Anyone who is engaged with computerized business is aware of the totalizing dominance that Windows has on our workday. It is responsible for everything we do online, from package management and installation to workflow optimization - it really seems like we're all on Outlook communicating with clients, on Teams to collaborate with our coworkers and management, or on Edge interacting with our web applications.

However, there are alternatives out there which should not be overlooked, especially in scenarios where every single dollar counts. Linux is a bundle of free operating systems that are built on similar architecture. Because so many distributions (versions) of Linux exist, it can be difficult for us to determine whether any particular distro is right for us. 

This series of articles is meant to briefly explain what makes each distribution of Linux unique, and whether they are worth setting up for users who are already comfortable with Windows environments.

If security, privacy, and control are priorities for your work, Parrot OS could be an excellent fit. It’s trusted by cybersecurity professionals, ethical hackers, and privacy-minded users who want to stay safe and in control.

![[parrot-desktop-6.0.09dbcbab.png]]

#### **1. Built for Security**  
Parrot OS is designed from the ground up for people who need top-notch security. It comes with powerful tools to protect your data, test your network security, and keep threats out — right out of the box.

#### **2. Privacy First**  
Parrot OS includes features that help protect your privacy online. Your personal and business data stays yours — no hidden tracking, no unwanted data collection.

![[01-parrot-linux-download.webp]]
#### **3. Free and Open Source**  
Like other Linux systems, Parrot OS is completely free. You don’t pay for licenses or subscriptions, and you’re free to use it on as many computers as you want.

#### **4. Great for Professionals**  
Parrot is popular with people who work in IT, cybersecurity, and digital forensics — but it’s also perfectly usable as a daily system for browsing, email, and office work. You get the security extras _and_ a system that works for everyday tasks.

![[parrot11.webp]]
#### **5. Up to Date and Reliable**  
Parrot OS stays updated with the latest security patches and improvements. Unlike Windows, updates are clear, predictable, and under your control — no surprise restarts or forced changes.

#### **6. Supportive Community**  
Parrot has an active community of cybersecurity enthusiasts and professionals. If you run into questions, you’ll find advice and help from people who really know what they’re doing.

![[really-enjoying-learning-linux-on-a-parrotos-virtual-machine-v0-02rm74cxjxt81.webp]]

---

**In short:** Parrot OS is ideal if you want a secure, private, and professional-grade system — without the cost and hassle of Windows licenses or constant worries about security risks. It’s a solid choice if your work involves sensitive data or you just want peace of mind.
