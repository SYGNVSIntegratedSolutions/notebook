### **Top 10 Easy Windows Hardening Steps for End-Users** ✨

1. **Keep Windows Updated**
    

- Turn on automatic Windows Updates to get the latest security patches.
    

2. **Use a Strong Password or Passphrase**
    

- Make it long, unique, and hard to guess. Avoid reusing passwords.
    

3. **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**
    

- Use MFA for your Microsoft account and other services where available.
    

4. **Turn On Windows Defender Firewall**
    

- Make sure it’s enabled to block unauthorized connections.
    

5. **Install Trusted Antivirus/Anti-Malware**
    

- Windows Defender is solid — just ensure it’s running and up to date.
    

6. **Use a Standard User Account for Daily Tasks**
    

- Avoid using an account with admin rights for everyday work.
    

7. **Encrypt Your Drive with BitLocker**
    

- If available, enable BitLocker to protect your data if your device is lost or stolen.
    

8. **Disable Unnecessary Startup Apps & Services**
    

- Fewer background apps reduce your attack surface and speed up your PC.
    

9. **Be Wary of Unknown USB Devices & Downloads**
    

- Only plug in trusted USB drives. Download software from reputable sources.
    

10. **Back Up Important Data Regularly**
    

- Use File History or OneDrive to back up your files — ransomware can’t win if you have backups.
    

---

**Bonus Tip:**  
Always lock your screen (Win + L) when stepping away, especially in public or shared spaces.
