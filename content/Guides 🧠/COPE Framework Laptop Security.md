## **Top 10 COPE Laptop Security Recommendations** 💻

1. **Use Strong, Unique Passwords**
    
    - Create complex passwords and change them regularly. Avoid sharing credentials.
        
2. **Keep the OS and Software Updated**
    
    - Apply updates and security patches as soon as they’re released.
        
3. **Use Company-Approved Antivirus/EDR**
    
    - Ensure company-managed antivirus or endpoint protection is installed and active.
        
4. **Encrypt the Hard Drive**
    
    - Enable full-disk encryption (e.g., BitLocker) to protect data if the laptop is lost or stolen.
        
5. **Connect Through VPN Offsite**
    
    - Use a secure company VPN when working remotely or on public networks.
        
6. **Store Files on Secure Drives**
    
    - Save work to company-approved cloud storage or network drives instead of local folders whenever possible.
        
7. **Be Cautious with External Devices**
    
    - Avoid plugging in unknown USB drives or devices. Scan all media for malware.
        
8. **Lock or Log Off When Away**
    
    - Lock your laptop (Win+L) when stepping away, even briefly.
        
9. **Back Up Data Securely**
    
    - Ensure backups run as scheduled or use approved cloud services for versioned backups.
        
10. **Report Incidents Quickly**
    

- Notify IT immediately about suspicious activity, lost laptops, or potential security breaches.
