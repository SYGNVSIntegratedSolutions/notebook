### ➡️ **Why Use Forms?**

Laserfiche Forms let you collect information online, route it for approvals ✅, and store it directly in Laserfiche — no more paper or email chaos! 🗂️✨

---

### 🏁 **A) Open Laserfiche Forms**

1️⃣ Open your web browser and log in to **Laserfiche Forms** (your link might look like: `forms.yourcompany.com`).  
2️⃣ Go to the **Management** or **Designer** page.

---

### ➕ **B) Create a New Process**

1️⃣ Click **Create Process**.  
2️⃣ Name it clearly — e.g., `Leave Request Form` or `Vendor Registration`.

---

### 📄 **C) Design the Form**

1️⃣ Click **New Form** — the drag-and-drop form builder opens.  
2️⃣ From the left panel, drag fields onto your form:

- 🧑 **Single Line**: for Name, Title, etc.
    
- 🗂️ **Dropdown**: for lists like Departments.
    
- 📅 **Date**: for picking a date.
    
- ✅ **Checkboxes**: for yes/no.
    
- 📝 **Paragraph**: for longer text.  
    3️⃣ Click each field to set:
    
- Label (what the user sees)
    
- Default value (optional)
    
- Required or optional
    

---

### 🧩 **D) Add Rules (Optional)**

Want to show/hide fields based on answers? 🔍  
1️⃣ Click the **Rules** tab.  
2️⃣ Add logic like: _If Department is IT → show extra fields_.  
3️⃣ Test your rules in the preview.

---

### 🔁 **E) Add an Approval Flow**

1️⃣ After the form, add **Steps** to your process:

- 📥 **Submit**: user fills out and submits the form.
    
- 👤 **User Task**: someone reviews it (e.g., Manager).
    
- ✅ **Approve/Reject**: define what happens next.  
    2️⃣ Assign each step to the right person/group.
    
- Example: `Manager` approves leave requests.
    

---

### ⚙️ **F) Link to Repository (Optional)**

Want the submitted form to save into a Laserfiche folder? 📂  
1️⃣ Add a **Service Task** at the end of your process.  
2️⃣ Configure it to **Save to Repository**.  
3️⃣ Pick the destination folder and map form fields to metadata.

---

### 🧪 **G) Test Your Form**

1️⃣ Click **Preview** to see how it looks.  
2️⃣ Submit a test entry.  
3️⃣ Make sure it routes correctly and saves where you want it.

---

### 🚀 **H) Publish and Share**

1️⃣ Click **Publish** to make your form live.  
2️⃣ Get the **Form Link** — copy it or embed it on your website or intranet.  
3️⃣ 🎉 Now anyone with permission can fill it out!

---

### 💡 **Tips for Good Forms**

📝 Keep forms short and clear — only ask for what you need.  
✅ Use required fields to ensure you get the info you need every time.  
📬 Use notifications to alert people when a form is submitted or approved.  
🔄 Combine forms + workflows for a fully automated process!
