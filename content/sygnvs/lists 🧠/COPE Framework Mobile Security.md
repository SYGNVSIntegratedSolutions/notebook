### **Top 10 COPE Mobile Device Security Recommendations** 📱

1. **Use Strong Passcodes and Biometrics**
    
    - Secure the device with a long PIN or password, plus Face ID or fingerprint unlock if available.
        
2. **Keep the OS and Apps Updated**
    
    - Install updates promptly to patch security vulnerabilities.
        
3. **Install Only Approved Apps**
    
    - Limit installations to company-approved apps via an MDM or managed app store.
        
4. **Use Separate Work and Personal Profiles**
    
    - If supported, separate company data with a secure work profile or container.
        
5. **Encrypt Device Storage**
    
    - Ensure full-disk encryption is enabled to protect data if the phone is lost or stolen.
        
6. **Use VPN When Offsite**
    
    - Connect through a company-approved VPN when accessing work resources on public Wi-Fi.
        
7. **Enable Remote Wipe**
    
    - Keep remote wipe and tracking (Find My Device) turned on through MDM.
        
8. **Be Vigilant Against Phishing**
    
    - Stay alert for suspicious texts, emails, and calls — verify before clicking links or sharing information.
        
9. **Report Lost or Compromised Devices Immediately**
    
    - Notify IT support right away so they can lock or wipe the device if needed.
        
10. **Lock Screen When Not in Use**
    

- Always lock the device when stepping away to prevent unauthorized access.
