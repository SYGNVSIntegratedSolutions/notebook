### **Top 10 iPhone Security Recommendations (BYOD)** 📱

1. **Use a Strong Passcode**
    
    - It’s recommended to set a long, complex passcode or use biometric security (Face ID or Touch ID) to lock your iPhone.
        
2. **Keep iOS Updated**
    
    - Regularly install the latest iOS updates to patch security flaws and keep your device protected.
        
3. **Enable Find My iPhone**
    
    - Turn on Find My iPhone to locate, lock, or erase your device remotely if it’s lost or stolen.
        
4. **Use Company-Approved Apps for Work**
    
    - Download and use only trusted apps approved by your IT team for accessing company email, files, and communication.
        
5. **Turn On Auto-Lock**
    
    - Set a short auto-lock time (e.g., 1–2 minutes) to automatically lock your phone when not in use.
        
6. **Use VPN for Work-Related Connections**
    
    - When accessing company data on public or unsecured Wi-Fi, it’s recommended to use a secure VPN.
        
7. **Enable iCloud Backup Securely**
    
    - Back up your device with encrypted iCloud backup — but avoid backing up sensitive work data to personal cloud accounts if your company restricts it.
        
8. **Be Cautious with Messages and Links**
    
    - Be alert for suspicious texts or emails (smishing/phishing) and verify unexpected requests before clicking links.
        
9. **Separate Work and Personal Accounts**
    
    - Use separate apps or profiles when possible to keep company data isolated from personal data.
        
10. **Notify IT if the Device is Lost or Compromised**
    

- Report lost devices, suspected breaches, or unusual activity to your IT support promptly so they can help protect company data.
### **Top 10 Android Security Recommendations (BYOD)**

1. **Use a Strong Screen Lock**
    
    - Set a strong PIN, password, or biometric lock (fingerprint or face) to keep your device secure.
        
2. **Keep Your OS and Apps Updated**
    
    - Regularly update your Android version and apps to patch known security issues.
        
3. **Enable Find My Device**
    
    - Turn on Google’s Find My Device to locate, lock, or wipe your phone remotely if it’s lost or stolen.
        
4. **Download Apps Only from Trusted Sources**
    
    - Stick to Google Play Store or your company’s approved app store — avoid sideloading apps from unknown websites.
        
5. **Turn On Google Play Protect**
    
    - Make sure Google Play Protect is enabled to scan apps for malware automatically.
        
6. **Use VPN for Work Data on Public Wi-Fi**
    
    - When connecting to public Wi-Fi, use a secure VPN for work-related activities.
        
7. **Encrypt Your Device**
    
    - Most modern Androids encrypt data by default — verify this is turned on in your security settings.
        
8. **Be Alert for Phishing and Malicious Apps**
    
    - Be cautious with links in emails, texts, or social apps — and watch out for fake apps pretending to be legitimate.
        
9. **Separate Work and Personal Data**
    
    - Use separate work profiles or mobile device management (MDM) tools if provided, to keep company data isolated.
        
10. **Report Lost Devices or Security Issues Immediately**
    

- Notify your IT team right away if your phone is lost, stolen, or you notice suspicious activity.
